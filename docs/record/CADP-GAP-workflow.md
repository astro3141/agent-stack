## D. Summary

1. **No effect client at all** (WP-01/04/07/11): agent-stack never allocates, seals, or submits anything to a CADP kernel; no kernel is even in its composition. The TD requires exactly this and nothing else — it is the one gap the TD expects closed for any governed claim, and it is 100% open.
2. **External effects are actuated by the workflow with its own credential** (WP-12/27): PRs are opened by the devflow package via a GitHub token in the agent's environment; WP §6 says that places every such effect outside Kernel Conformance. The TD expects this closed (PEP-held credential), and agent-stack explicitly declines to ("Nothing in the platform deduplicates an external effect… and nothing should", OPERATIONS §38).
3. **Evidence is telemetry, not K2** (WP-18/19/20): `evidence_index.json` + MLflow; reviews are the P0 relayed-review case and would be `SELF_REPORT`. The TD expects K2 submission for anything a gate consumes; it does NOT expect candidate-producer provenance as K2 (WP-21 — agent-stack is consistent there).
4. **Non-enrollment is correct and mutually agreed** (WP-24/25): the TD does not expect enrollment closed; agent-stack's F21 shows two of three revisit conditions now hold on v0.1.37 and the third is categorical. The TD's stated §5.1 basis is partly stale and should be restated on the K7/target-authority ground.
5. **Scope fence is honoured** (WP-29): agent-stack keeps DAG/retry/checkpoint/roles internal and asks CADP for nothing — the D0 lesson holds, but only because the two systems do not touch at all.

Net: agent-stack is a Workflow Plane with no boundary contact; the TD expects gaps 1–3 closed by any client claiming per-effect Kernel Conformance, and expects gap 4 to stay open. No WP §7 control is runnable against agent-stack today.
